Key ideas to know

  1. Earth's outer layer is broken into tectonic plates that slowly move. Plate boundaries are divergent (move apart), convergent (move together), or transform (slide past).
  2. Ocean crust is made at mid-ocean ridges (divergent boundaries) and destroyed at subduction zones (convergent boundaries), where it sinks into the mantle.
  3. Seafloor rock gets older with distance from a ridge, on both sides. This pattern shows the seafloor spreads.
  4. Ocean crust is young (under about 200 million years old) because it is constantly recycled. Continental crust is older (up to about 4,000 million years) because it is less dense and is not recycled as easily.
  5. Continents grew outward from ancient cores as plate collisions added newer crust around the edges.
  6. Strong evidence comes from several independent lines that agree: GPS (now), seafloor ages and magnetic stripes (past), and matching fossils and rocks (past).

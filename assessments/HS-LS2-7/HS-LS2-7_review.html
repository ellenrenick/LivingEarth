Key ideas to know

  1. Human activities affect ecosystems: deforestation removes habitat, pollution harms organisms, dams block migration, and invasive species can crowd out natives.
  2. Start a design with evidence. Use data to find the biggest cause of a problem and target it.
  3. Criteria are the goals a solution must meet. Constraints are the limits, such as cost and time.
  4. Trade-offs: a solution can gain something and cost something. Compare gains and costs with numbers.
  5. Refine with feedback and new data. If a test shows a weakness, change the design and test it again.
  6. A strong defense uses evidence about how well the solution works and what it costs, and honestly names a limit.

Conservation of matter: In any change, including the changes inside living things, atoms are rearranged into new molecules. Atoms are never created, never destroyed, and never changed into a different element.

Part 2: Reading a formula

A formula tells you which elements are in a molecule and how many atoms of each. The small number comes after the element it counts. No number means 1.

Example: C6H12O6 has 6 carbon, 12 hydrogen, and 6 oxygen atoms.

Open the Leaf Atom Lab (link on Canvas): https://claude.ai/artifact/JyQjAzmqPp4jY1ef52tkvp. Atom colors: black = carbon (C), white = hydrogen (H), red = oxygen (O).

Round 1

1. Count the atoms in the 6 CO2 and 6 H2O molecules. Fill in the Before row below and in the lab, then press Check my counts.
2. Turn on LIGHT ENERGY. The leaf can only rearrange atoms while the light is on.
3. Press Take the molecules apart. Tap atoms to rebuild them into 1 glucose (C6H12O6) and 6 O2.

sugar (C, H, O) + N from soil → amino acids → proteins → new cells → a bigger plant
  1. The no-nitrogen plants still made sugar. They made more: 25 vs. 12 mg/g.
  2. But sugar has only C, H, and O. Amino acids need nitrogen, so they made less protein: 10 vs. 40 mg/g.
  3. No new protein means no new cells, so they stayed small: 11 vs. 32 cm and 2.4 vs. 7.5 g.
  4. The unused sugar built up in the leaves.

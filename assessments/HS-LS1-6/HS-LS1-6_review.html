Key ideas to know

  1. Sugar is made of only carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen. Plants build sugar from CO2 and water using light energy. Light is energy, not matter.
  2. Amino acids also contain nitrogen (some contain sulfur). Nitrogen and sulfur are not in sugar, so they come from somewhere else, such as the soil.
  3. Atoms are conserved. They are rearranged, never created, destroyed, or changed into another element.
  4. Atoms from sugar are rearranged and combined with other elements to build larger molecules.
  5. Matter moves through food chains. An animal breaks food down and rebuilds its atoms into its own molecules.
  6. Scientists revise explanations when new evidence does not fit.

Write It

Write an explanation: Claim, Evidence (at least two numbers), and Reasoning (how atoms from sugar combine with other elements to build the molecules needed to grow). Then predict what happens if sulfur is added to Field 2.

Check your writing. Did you:

  • States that the lack of sulfur limited building proteins even though sugar was made
  • Uses at least two numbers (sugar and protein or yield)
  • Says sugar has only C, H, and O, and sulfur has to come from the soil
  • Says atoms from sugar are rearranged and combined with sulfur (and other elements) to build the molecules for growth
  • Explains why sugar built up and predicts sugar drops and protein and yield rise after adding sulfur

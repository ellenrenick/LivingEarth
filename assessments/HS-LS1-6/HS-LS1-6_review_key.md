## Section B: Atom accounting

1. **The fat molecule.** It contains only C, H, and O, the same elements as glucose.
2. **Serine** needs nitrogen. **Methionine** needs nitrogen and sulfur.
3. From the soil, usually as dissolved nitrogen and sulfur compounds that the roots absorb. Fertilizer and nitrogen-fixing bacteria are also acceptable answers.
4. Atoms are conserved. They can be rearranged, but one element can't change into another. Nitrogen must be added from an outside source.
5. Amino acids, and therefore proteins, contain nitrogen, but sugar doesn't. Fertilizer supplies the nitrogen the plant combines with atoms from sugar to make amino acids, so more protein can be built.

## Section D: Trace the atoms

1. carbon dioxide (CO2)
2. light; sugar (glucose)
3. nitrogen; amino acids
4. proteins
5. breaks down (digests)

**Think:** The *atoms* come from the clover, but the rabbit breaks clover proteins down into amino acids and then links them in a *new order* to make its own proteins. The atoms are the same, but they're arranged into different molecules.

## Section G: Revise an explanation

1. The tree gained about 75 kg, but the soil lost only about 0.06 kg, so almost none of the tree's mass came from the soil.
2. **Sample revision:** "My original idea was that the tree ate the soil, but the soil lost only 0.06 kg while the tree gained about 75 kg. The carbon in the tree came from carbon dioxide in the air. The tree used light energy to build sugar from CO2 and water. Then the atoms from sugar were rearranged and combined with small amounts of nitrogen and other elements from the soil to make amino acids, proteins, and the other molecules in wood and leaves. The soil lost only a tiny amount because it supplied only these few extra elements, not the carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen that make up most of the tree."

Score the revision with the Section F rubric. A 4 needs CO2 as the carbon source, light as energy (not matter), sugar as the starting material, and soil elements combined with sugar's atoms.

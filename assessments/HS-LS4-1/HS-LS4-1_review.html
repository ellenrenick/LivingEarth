Key ideas to know

  1. Common ancestry means different species descend from earlier species they share. Evolution is a change in the inherited traits of a population over many generations.
  2. DNA similarity: the more closely related two species are, the more of their DNA is alike.
  3. Body structures: homologous structures (like the bones in a human arm and a whale flipper) share a pattern inherited from a common ancestor. Vestigial structures are leftovers from ancestors.
  4. Embryos: related species have similar early development, even when adults look different.
  5. The fossil record shows living things changing across rock layers of different ages, including intermediate forms.
  6. Several independent lines of evidence that agree make the strongest case. Species evolve from shared ancestors, not from each other.

  1. What does each of the three observations tell you about whale ancestry?

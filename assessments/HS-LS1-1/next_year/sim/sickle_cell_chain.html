Sickle Cell Chain

    1. One base in the hemoglobin gene

    The start of the hemoglobin gene, read three bases at a time. Switch genes and find the one letter that changes amino acid 6 (of 146).

    2. The changed hemoglobin sticks together

    Red blood cells are packed with hemoglobin, the protein that carries oxygen. Lower the oxygen and watch the two kinds of hemoglobin.

    3. Round cells flow, sickle cells jam

    A small blood vessel. Round, bendy cells squeeze through. Stiff sickle cells catch on each other and block the flow, so less oxygen reaches the body.

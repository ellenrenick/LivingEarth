    1. One letter in the gene

    The gene for a pigment-making protein (tyrosinase) is read three bases at a time. Switch between the two cats and watch one triplet.

    Made-up neighbors around the real change: in Siamese cats one letter swaps Gly (glycine) for Arg (arginine).

    4. The whole chain

    DNAOne letter in the pigment gene is different: GGA becomes AGA.
    Amino acidThe protein's chain has Arg where it used to have Gly.
    ProteinThe changed protein works only in skin cooler than about 33 °C.
    CellsEvery cell has the same DNA, but only pigment cells use this gene.
    BodyEars, face, paws, and tail are cool, so only they grow dark fur. A kitten is born white because it was warm all over before birth.

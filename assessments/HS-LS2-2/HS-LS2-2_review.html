Key ideas to know

  1. A population is one species in one area. Biodiversity is the variety of species in an ecosystem.
  2. Factors that change them include predators, pollution, habitat loss, invasive species, and food supply.
  3. Mean (average): add the values and divide by how many there are. Rate of change: (end value - start value) ÷ time.
  4. A trend is the general direction of the data: rising, falling, or level.
  5. To compare ecosystems of different sizes, divide by area (for example, species per km2).
  6. New data can change an explanation. Compare groups that differ in only one factor to see which factor matters.

## LS2-2.1 (DOK 1, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can define population and biodiversity and name factors that change them.

**Q1.** A population is

A. all the species in an area  
B. a group of different species  
C. all the living and nonliving things in an area  
D. all the individuals of one species in the same area  ✅  

*Answer D.* A population is one species in one area.

**Q2.** Biodiversity is the

A. variety of different species in an ecosystem  ✅  
B. number of individuals of one species  
C. amount of rainfall  
D. size of an ecosystem  

*Answer A.* Biodiversity is the variety of species.

**Q3.** Which is a factor that can change a population or biodiversity?

A. A student's opinion  
B. A new predator, pollution, or loss of habitat  ✅  
C. The name of a lake  
D. The color of a rock  

*Answer B.* Predators, pollution, and habitat loss are examples of factors that change populations.

**Q1.** | Year | 1 | 2 | 3 |

How many frogs were counted in year 2?

A. 50  
B. 90

**Q2.** The table above shows that the number of frogs

A. increased each year  ✅  
B. decreased each year  
C. was random  
D. stayed the same  

*Answer A.* 50, 70, 90 is an increase.

**Q3.** Describe in 1 sentence what the data in the table show, using numbers.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | States the direction of change with at least two values. |
| 1 | States the direction without numbers. |

*Model answer:* The frog population increased from 50 in year 1 to 90 in year 3.

## LS2-2.3 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can find averages, rates of change, and trends in population data.

**Q1.** What is the mean of 10, 14, 12, and 16?

A. 12  
B. 52  
C. 16  
D. 13  ✅  

*Answer D.* (10 + 14 + 12 + 16) / 4 = 13.

**Q2.** A population grew from 200 to 260 in 3 years. What is the rate of change?

A. 20 per year  ✅  
B. 260 per year  
C. 3 per year  
D. 60 per year  

*Answer A.* (260 - 200) / 3 = 20 per year.

**Q3.** Data: 80, 70, 60, 50, 40. Name the trend and give the average change per time period.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Decreasing trend; change of -10 per period. |
| 1 | Trend or change correct, not both. |
| 0 | Blank or incorrect. |

*Model answer:* The trend is decreasing, by 10 each time period.

## LS2-2.4 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can use math to compare populations and biodiversity across ecosystems of different sizes.

**Q1.** Park A has 30 species in 3 km2. Park B has 50 species in 10 km2. Which has more species per km2?

A. They are equal  
B. Cannot be compared  
C. Park B: 5 versus 10  
D. Park A: 10 per km<sup>2</sup> versus 5  ✅  

*Answer D.* 30 / 3 = 10; 50 / 10 = 5.

**Q2.** Why divide by area when comparing two ecosystems of different sizes?

A. So the comparison is fair for the size of each ecosystem  ✅  
B. To find the mean  
C. To make numbers bigger  
D. Because area does not matter  

*Answer A.* Dividing by area gives a fair comparison.

**Q3.** Pond P has 15 fish in 0.5 km2. Pond Q has 36 fish in 3 km2. Show which pond has more fish per km2.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Pond P: 30 per km<sup>2</sup>; Pond Q: 12 per km<sup>2</sup>; concludes P has more. |
| 1 | One value correct, or correct method with an arithmetic error. |
| 0 | Blank or incorrect. |

*Model answer:* Pond P: 15 / 0.5 = 30 per km<sup>2</sup>. Pond Q: 36 / 3 = 12 per km<sup>2</sup>. Pond P has more fish for its size.

## LS2-2.5 (DOK 3, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can use math as evidence to explain how a factor affects a population or biodiversity.

**Q1.** | Pollution level | Low | Medium | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Insect species | 40 | 25 | 10 |

Which statement uses the data correctly?

A. Insect species fell by 10%.  
B. Insect species increased with pollution.  
C. Pollution had no effect.  
D. Insect species fell from 40 to 10 as pollution rose, a 75% decrease.  ✅  

*Answer D.* (40 - 10) / 40 = 75%.

**Q2.** A claim that a factor affects a population is strongest when

A. numbers show a change that goes with the factor  ✅  
B. people believe it  
C. it sounds reasonable  
D. only one example is given  

*Answer A.* Numbers that change with a factor are good evidence.

**Q3.** Use the table above: write one sentence explaining how pollution affects biodiversity and include a calculation.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | States that higher pollution lowers species and shows a correct calculation. |
| 1 | States the relationship without a calculation, or a calculation without the explanation. |
| 0 | Blank or incorrect. |

*Model answer:* As pollution rises, insect biodiversity falls: from 40 to 10 species, which is a 75% drop.

## LS2-2.6 (DOK 3, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can use new data to revise my explanation of what affects a population or biodiversity.

**Q1.** A student says deer decreased because of cold winters. New data show deer also decreased in mild winters, but only where hunting was allowed. What should she do?

A. Keep her explanation unchanged.  
B. Ignore the new data.  
C. Say the data are wrong.  
D. Revise her explanation to include hunting.  ✅  

*Answer D.* Explanations should change when new data do not fit.

**Q2.** Which revised explanation best fits the new data above?

A. Deer decreased mainly where hunting was allowed, in both cold and mild winters.  ✅  
B. Deer decreased only because of cold winters.  
C. Hunting increased the deer population.  
D. Deer did not decrease.  

*Answer A.* The new data point to hunting.

**Q3.** Your first explanation: “Fish decreased because of a drought.” New data: fish also decreased 70% in lakes with no drought but with a new invasive predator. Revise your explanation in 1 or 2 sentences.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Revises to include the predator, using the new data. |
| 1 | Mentions the predator but keeps the old explanation unchanged, or revises without using the data. |
| 0 | Blank or no revision. |

*Model answer:* Fish decreased because of the invasive predator as well as drought: lakes with no drought but with the predator still lost 70% of their fish.

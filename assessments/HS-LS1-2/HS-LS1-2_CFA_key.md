## LS1-2.1 (DOK 1, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can name the levels of organization in an organism, from cells to the whole organism.

**Q1.** Which list shows the levels of organization from smallest to largest?

A. Tissue, cell, organ, organism, organ system  
B. Organ, tissue, cell, organ system, organism  
C. Organism, organ system, organ, tissue, cell  
D. Cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism  ✅  

*Answer D.* Cells make tissues, tissues make organs, organs make organ systems, and organ systems make the organism.

**Q2.** A group of similar cells that work together to do one job is called a

A. tissue  ✅  
B. organ system  
C. organism  
D. organ  

*Answer A.* Similar cells working together make a tissue.

**Q3.** The stomach, intestines, and liver work together to digest food. Together they are an example of a(n)

A. organ  
B. organ system  ✅  
C. cell  
D. tissue  

*Answer B.* A group of organs working together is an organ system. Each one alone is an organ.

## LS1-2.2 (DOK 1, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can state that all living things are made of cells, the basic unit of structure and function.

**Q1.** Which statement about cells is true for **every** living thing?

A. It is made of many tissues and organs.  
B. It is made of exactly one cell.  
C. It has a brain and a heart.  
D. It is made of one or more cells.  ✅  

*Answer D.* Every living thing is made of at least one cell. Many have no organs at all.

**Q2.** A bacterium is made of a single cell. Which statement is correct?

A. That one cell carries out all the functions of life for the bacterium.  ✅  
B. It is a tissue, not an organism.  
C. It depends on organs to carry out its functions.  
D. It is not living because it has no tissues.  

*Answer A.* A single cell can be a whole organism and carry out the functions of life.

**Q3.** The cell is called the basic unit of life because

A. it is made of many organ systems  
B. it is the smallest part that can carry out the functions of life and it makes up all living things  ✅  
C. it is found only in animals  
D. it is the largest structure in an organism  

*Answer B.* Cells are the smallest units of structure and function in living things.

## LS1-2.3 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can describe how each level is built from the level below it.

**Q1.** Which sentence correctly describes how the levels are built?

A. Cells are made of tissues, and tissues are made of organs.  
B. Organs are made of cells, and tissues are made of organs.  
C. Organ systems are made of tissues, and organs are made of organ systems.  
D. Tissues are made of cells, and organs are made of tissues.  ✅  

*Answer D.* Each level is built from the level below: cells, tissues, organs, organ systems.

**Q2.** The stomach contains muscle tissue, a lining tissue, and nervous tissue. Why is the stomach called an **organ**?

A. It is made of several different tissues that work together to do a job.  ✅  
B. It is made of several organ systems.  
C. It is made of only one kind of cell.  
D. It is the largest part of the digestive system.  

*Answer A.* An organ is made of different tissues working together.

**Q3.** In 2 or 3 sentences, describe how the **digestive system** is built up from cells. Name each level in order.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Names cells, tissues, organs, and the system in the correct order and says each is made of the level before it. |
| 1 | Names the levels but misses one or gets the order or the building relationship partly wrong. |
| 0 | Blank or the levels are in the wrong order. |

*Model answer:* Cells that are alike, such as stomach lining cells, make up a tissue. Different tissues, like muscle and lining tissue, make up an organ such as the stomach. Several organs, such as the stomach and intestines, make up the digestive system.

## LS1-2.4 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can explain how a part's structure fits its job, and how an organism's systems work together.

**Q1.** The air sacs in the lungs have very thin walls. How does this structure fit the job of the lungs?

A. Thin walls keep air from entering the lungs.  
B. Thin walls make the lungs heavier.  
C. Thin walls are not related to what the lungs do.  
D. Thin walls let oxygen pass quickly into the blood.  ✅  

*Answer D.* The thin walls fit the job of passing oxygen to the blood.

**Q2.** Which statement best shows two organ systems working together?

A. The respiratory system takes in oxygen and the circulatory system carries it to the body.  ✅  
B. The respiratory system takes in oxygen and then does the work of the circulatory system.  
C. The circulatory system works alone because it does not depend on other systems.  
D. The skeletal system and the respiratory system make the same product.  

*Answer A.* Systems depend on one another: one brings oxygen in and the other delivers it.

**Q3.** Choose one organ (for example, the small intestine, a leaf, or the heart). Explain how its **structure** fits its **function** in 1 or 2 sentences.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Names a real structure and the function and explains how the structure helps the function. |
| 1 | Names a structure and function but does not explain the connection. |
| 0 | Blank, or the structure and function do not match. |

*Model answer:* The small intestine has many tiny folds, which give it a large surface area, so more digested food can pass into the blood.

## LS1-2.5 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can use a model to show how an organism's systems work together to do one job, like moving.

**Q1.** A model of a jump is: *brain (nervous) → leg muscles (muscular) → leg bones (skeletal) → body moves*. What does this model show?

A. Three organs that each do the job alone.  
B. Three cells that make a tissue.  
C. That bones move without muscles.  
D. Three organ systems pass information and force along to do one job.  ✅  

*Answer D.* The arrows show systems interacting: the nervous system signals, the muscles pull, and the bones move.

**Q2.** Which part is **missing** from this model of a runner's response? *Brain signals → leg muscles contract → legs move.*

A. The skeletal system, because muscles pull on bones to move the legs  ✅  
B. Nothing; muscles can move the legs without bones  
C. The tissue level, because every model must list tissues  
D. The digestive system, because it always controls movement  

*Answer A.* Muscles move the body by pulling on bones.

**Q3.** Use the model *nervous system → muscular system → skeletal system → hand moves*. What would happen if the first link, the nervous system, stopped working? Explain in 1 or 2 sentences.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Predicts that the muscles would not get a signal and the hand would not move, using the arrows in the model. |
| 1 | Predicts the hand would not move but does not connect it to the model. |
| 0 | Blank or incorrect. |

*Model answer:* The muscles would not get the signal to contract, so they would not pull on the bones and the hand would not move.

## LS1-2.6 (DOK 3, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can build my own model showing how levels and systems in an organism work together to carry out functions.

**Q1.** A student's model shows only the heart. Which change makes it a better model of how the **circulatory system** works with other systems?

A. Make the heart bigger.  
B. Label the heart as a tissue.  
C. Remove the arrows so the model looks simpler.  
D. Add blood vessels, the lungs, and arrows showing how blood carries oxygen to the muscles.  ✅  

*Answer D.* A good model shows parts and how they connect and interact.

**Q2.** Which model best shows **levels** of organization?

A. Muscle cell → muscle tissue → biceps (organ) → muscular system → organism  ✅  
B. Biceps → muscle cell → muscular system → muscle tissue  
C. Muscle tissue → muscle cell → muscular system → biceps  
D. Muscular system → biceps → muscle cell → muscle tissue  

*Answer A.* The levels climb in order: cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism.

**Q3.** You are building a model of how an organism **digests food and delivers it to the body**. List three parts you will include and describe how two of them connect (1 or 2 sentences).

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Lists three real parts or systems and correctly describes how two connect. |
| 1 | Lists three parts, but the connection is missing or only partly correct. |
| 0 | Blank or the parts do not belong to the function. |

*Model answer:* Parts: stomach, small intestine, circulatory system. The small intestine passes digested food into the blood, and the circulatory system carries it to the cells of the body.

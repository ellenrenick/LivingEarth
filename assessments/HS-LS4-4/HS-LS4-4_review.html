Key ideas to know

  1. Natural selection is when individuals with helpful inherited traits are more likely to survive and reproduce. An adaptation is such a trait.
  2. It needs three things: variation in a trait, the trait is inherited, and some versions help survival or reproduction more than others.
  3. Populations evolve, not individuals. Traits gained during a lifetime (like stretched necks) are not passed on.
  4. Biotic factors are living (predators, food, competitors). Abiotic factors are nonliving (temperature, acidity, light, rainfall).
  5. Data show evolution as a shift in how common a trait is (its allele frequency or percent) over generations.
  6. If the environment changes, different traits are favored. The population becomes better suited to the new conditions.

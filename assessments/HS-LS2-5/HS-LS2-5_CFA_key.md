## LS2-5.1 (DOK 1, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can name the four spheres and where carbon is stored in each one.

**Q1.** Which list names the four spheres of Earth's systems?

A. Biosphere, stratosphere, lithosphere, ionosphere  
B. Atmosphere, ocean, forest, mountain  
C. Hydrosphere, geosphere, sunlight, biosphere  
D. Biosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, geosphere  ✅  

*Answer D.* The four spheres are the biosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, and geosphere.

**Q2.** Coal, oil, and natural gas store carbon in which sphere?

A. Geosphere  ✅  
B. Hydrosphere  
C. Biosphere only  
D. Atmosphere  

*Answer A.* Fossil fuels are part of the solid Earth, the geosphere.

**Q3.** Carbon dissolved in the ocean is stored in which sphere?

A. Geosphere  
B. Hydrosphere  ✅  
C. Biosphere  
D. Atmosphere  

*Answer B.* The ocean is part of the hydrosphere.

## LS2-5.2 (DOK 1, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can name processes besides photosynthesis and respiration that move carbon, like burning and burial in rock.

**Q1.** Burning gasoline in a car moves carbon from the

A. biosphere to the hydrosphere  
B. atmosphere to the biosphere  
C. hydrosphere to the geosphere  
D. geosphere (fossil fuel) to the atmosphere  ✅  

*Answer D.* Burning fossil fuels releases carbon from the geosphere into the air as CO2.

**Q2.** Dead sea creatures sink and are slowly buried under layers of mud that become rock. Which process is this?

A. Burial, which moves carbon from the biosphere into the geosphere  ✅  
B. Photosynthesis  
C. Burning  
D. Cellular respiration  

*Answer A.* Burial over long times stores carbon in rock.

**Q3.** When dead leaves rot, microbes break them down and carbon returns to the air and soil. This process is called

A. burial  
B. decay (decomposition)  ✅  
C. dissolving  
D. photosynthesis  

*Answer B.* Decomposers break down dead matter and return carbon to the air and soil.

## LS2-5.3 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can describe how photosynthesis moves carbon from air and water into living things, and respiration moves it back.

**Q1.** Which statement correctly describes photosynthesis?

A. It moves carbon from rock into the ocean.  
B. It releases carbon from sugar back into the atmosphere.  
C. It creates new carbon atoms.  
D. It uses light energy to move carbon from the atmosphere into sugar in living things.  ✅  

*Answer D.* Photosynthesis moves carbon from CO2 in the air into sugar.

**Q2.** Which statement correctly describes cellular respiration?

A. It releases the energy stored in sugar and returns carbon to the atmosphere as CO2.  ✅  
B. It builds sugar from CO2.  
C. It happens only in plants.  
D. It happens only in the dark.  

*Answer A.* Respiration happens in plant and animal cells all the time and gives off CO2.

**Q3.** In 2 sentences, describe how photosynthesis and cellular respiration move carbon in opposite directions between the atmosphere and living things.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Says photosynthesis moves carbon from air into living things and respiration moves it back to air. |
| 1 | Describes one process correctly and the other incompletely. |
| 0 | Blank or directions reversed. |

*Model answer:* Photosynthesis moves carbon from CO2 in the air into sugar in plants. Cellular respiration releases carbon from sugar back to the air as CO2.

## LS2-5.4 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can trace one carbon atom through at least three spheres and name each process that moves it.

**Q1.** Which path correctly traces a carbon atom from CO2 in the air to a fossil fuel?

A. Geosphere → atmosphere → biosphere  
B. Atmosphere → geosphere → biosphere  
C. Biosphere → hydrosphere → atmosphere only  
D. Atmosphere → biosphere (photosynthesis) → geosphere (burial over millions of years)  ✅  

*Answer D.* Photosynthesis moves carbon into living things; burial over time moves it into the geosphere as fossil fuel.

**Q2.** A carbon atom in a dead fish sinks to the seafloor and is buried. Which two spheres has it moved between?

A. Biosphere to geosphere (through the hydrosphere)  ✅  
B. Geosphere to atmosphere  
C. Hydrosphere to atmosphere only  
D. Atmosphere to biosphere  

*Answer A.* The fish was part of the biosphere, sank through the hydrosphere, and was buried in the geosphere.

**Q3.** Trace one carbon atom through **three different spheres**. Name the sphere at each step and the process that moved it.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Three different spheres in a logical order and a correct process named for each move. |
| 1 | Three spheres with a missing or wrong process, or two spheres with correct processes. |
| 0 | Blank or the path is not logical. |

*Model answer:* Atmosphere (CO2) to biosphere (sugar in a leaf) by photosynthesis; then the leaf is eaten, and an animal gives off CO2 by respiration (back to the atmosphere), or the leaf is buried to become part of the geosphere.

## LS2-5.5 (DOK 2, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can use a model to predict what happens to carbon elsewhere when one part of the cycle changes.

**Q1.** In a carbon cycle model, photosynthesis by ocean algae drops sharply. Which prediction is best?

A. The amount of carbon in the atmosphere will not be affected.  
B. More carbon will leave the air, because there are fewer algae.  
C. Carbon will be destroyed.  
D. Less carbon leaves the air and the surface water, so more carbon stays in the atmosphere.  ✅  

*Answer D.* When a process that removes carbon slows, carbon builds up in the sphere it would have left.

**Q2.** A model shows that burning fossil fuels adds more carbon to the atmosphere each year. What does the model predict for the ocean, which takes in CO2 from the air?

A. The ocean takes in more carbon.  ✅  
B. The ocean is not part of the model.  
C. The ocean gives off all its carbon.  
D. The ocean takes in less carbon.  

*Answer A.* More CO2 in the air means more dissolves into the ocean.

**Q3.** Use a carbon cycle model to predict what happens to the atmosphere when many dead plants and animals stop being buried. Explain in 1 or 2 sentences.

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Predicts a change in the atmosphere (more carbon stays in the air or less is stored in rock) and links it to the changed arrow. |
| 1 | Predicts a change but does not link it to the model. |
| 0 | Blank or incorrect. |

*Model answer:* Less carbon would be stored in the geosphere, so more would stay cycling through living things and the air, and the carbon in the atmosphere could rise.

## LS2-5.6 (DOK 3, 6 points)

**Learning target:** I can build a model showing how photosynthesis and respiration cycle carbon among all four spheres.

**Q1.** Which model is **most complete** as a model of the carbon cycle?

A. Arrows between plants and animals only  
B. Only an arrow from the air to plants  
C. Only the Sun and the air  
D. Arrows for photosynthesis (air to plants), respiration (living things to air), dissolving in the ocean, burial in rock, and burning fossil fuels  ✅  

*Answer D.* A good model includes all four spheres and the processes that connect them.

**Q2.** In a good model, the arrow for **cellular respiration** should point

A. from the biosphere to the atmosphere  ✅  
B. from the atmosphere to the biosphere  
C. from the Sun to the biosphere  
D. from the geosphere to the hydrosphere  

*Answer A.* Respiration returns carbon from living things to the air as CO2.

**Q3.** Plan your model. List the four spheres and write the arrows you will draw for **photosynthesis** and **cellular respiration** (name the spheres each arrow connects).

| Score | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | All four spheres named and both arrows correctly directed. |
| 1 | Three spheres or one arrow wrong or missing. |
| 0 | Blank or both arrows wrong. |

*Model answer:* Spheres: biosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, geosphere. Photosynthesis: atmosphere to biosphere. Cellular respiration: biosphere to atmosphere.

# HS-ESS2-7 Review: Answer Key

Use with `HS-ESS2-7_review.html`. Students do it on the review day, after the CFAs and before the CSA. Plan on 30 to 40 minutes. The Vocabulary and Write It sections are for everyone; students pick the other sections by their CFA scores (below 4 of 6).

## Vocabulary

1. biosphere
2. atmosphere
3. weathering
4. feedback
5. fossil
6. coevolution

## Section A: Earth changes life and life changes Earth

1. Life changed Earth.
2. Earth changed life.
3. Earth changed life.
4. Life changed Earth.
5. Example: life changed Earth when corals built reefs that changed erosion; Earth changed life when a colder climate caused extinctions.

## Section B: Chains of cause and effect

1. (b), (c), (a).
2. (b) and (c) are life changing Earth's atmosphere and rocks. (a) is the changed Earth making new life possible.
3. An Earth event changed the climate and life; survivors then evolved to fill open habitats.

## Section C: Writing an argument

1. Living things help form soil, and more life goes with deeper soil.
2. Site 1 has no life and 0 cm of soil; Site 3 has trees and 35 cm.
3. Microbes, lichens, and plants break down rock and add organic material that builds soil.

## Section D: Combining evidence

1. (b) and (c) show life changing the atmosphere (with (a) showing its effect on rocks). (d) shows the new atmosphere making large animals possible.
2. Microbes that made oxygen existed (b), oxygen rose in the air (c) and rusted iron in rocks (a), and later large animals that need oxygen appeared (d), so Earth and life changed together.
3. Likely no large animals, only small or one-celled life, because oxygen-using life needs oxygen.

## Section E: Feedbacks and the whole story

1. Life builds soil, soil supports more life, and more life builds more soil.
2. Corals (life) changed the coast (Earth), and the changed coast created habitat for more life.
3. Photosynthetic microbes added oxygen to the air, which changed rocks and later made animals possible. Each change shaped the next, so the two changed together.

## Write It

**Checklist (what a strong answer includes):**

- States that the spread of land plants and changes in soil and atmosphere went together
- Uses at least three numbers from the table
- Explains life changing Earth (plants building soil and changing the atmosphere)
- Explains Earth changing life (more soil and a changed climate let more kinds of life live on land)
- Links the steps in a chain and avoids saying more than the data show

**Model answer:** Life and Earth changed together as land plants spread. Land plants went from none to large forests, soil thickness rose from 1 to 10, and carbon dioxide in the air fell from 4,000 to 800 ppm. Plants and their roots break down rock and add organic material, which builds soil, and the data show CO<sub>2</sub> falling as plants spread, so life changed the atmosphere and the land. The thicker soil and changed air then changed conditions for life, letting more kinds of plants and animals live on land, which in turn built even more soil.

Key ideas to know

  1. Earth changes life. Ice ages, impacts, and changes in the atmosphere have caused extinctions and opened new habitats.
  2. Life changes Earth. Photosynthetic microbes added oxygen to the atmosphere. Plants and microbes build soil. Corals build reefs that change coasts.
  3. The changes link up in chains. Life added oxygen, oxygen changed rocks and weathering, and the new atmosphere made oxygen-using animals possible.
  4. A feedback is a loop. Life builds soil, soil supports more life, and more life builds more soil.
  5. Evidence comes from several records: fossils (life), rock chemistry and rusted iron layers (atmosphere and oceans), and climate records.
  6. A strong argument combines several kinds of evidence into one story with a clear claim and reasoning.

Section A: Earth changes life and life changes Earth

Label each example as Earth changed life or life changed Earth.

  1. Photosynthetic microbes added oxygen to the atmosphere.
  2. An ice age made large parts of the land too cold for many plants.
  3. An asteroid impact darkened the sky and many animals died out.
  4. Plants and lichens broke down rock and formed soil.
  5. Give one example of each direction in your own words.

Section B: Chains of cause and effect

Put the events in order and explain the links.

  1. Order: (a) oxygen-using animals appear, (b) microbes release oxygen, (c) oxygen builds up in the air and rusts iron in rocks.
  2. Which steps show life changing Earth, and which show Earth changing life?
  3. An asteroid impact cooled the climate and many animals died out. Small mammals later became many new kinds. Explain the two-way story.

Section D: Combining evidence

Four kinds of evidence: (a) rusted iron layers in rocks, (b) fossils of oxygen-making microbes, (c) gas trapped in ancient rocks showing rising oxygen, (d) fossils of large animals that appear later.

  1. Which kinds of evidence show life changed the atmosphere? Which show the changed atmosphere changed life?
  2. Write a combined argument in 3 sentences using at least three of the four.
  3. Extension: What might the fossil record look like if oxygen had never risen? Explain.

Section E: Feedbacks and the whole story

Use feedback loops and several examples to argue that Earth and life changed together.

  1. Describe a feedback between life and soil.
  2. A coral reef changes waves and erosion and also creates habitat. Explain why this is two-way.
  3. Write 2 sentences arguing Earth and life changed together, using one example.

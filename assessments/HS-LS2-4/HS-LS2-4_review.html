Key ideas to know

  1. Producers make their own food from sunlight, consumers eat other organisms, and decomposers break down dead matter and return it to the soil and air.
  2. Matter cycles. Atoms such as carbon are reused again and again. They are conserved.
  3. Energy flows one way. It enters as sunlight and leaves as heat. Ecosystems need a constant supply of new energy.
  4. The 10% rule: about 10% of the energy at one trophic level is stored in the next. The rest is used for life processes or lost as heat.
  5. To find the percent passed on: divide the energy in the next level by the energy in the level below, then multiply by 100.
  6. To show matter is conserved, add up all the parts. The total stays the same even when the parts change.

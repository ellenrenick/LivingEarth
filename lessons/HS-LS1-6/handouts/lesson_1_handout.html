Lesson 1: The Giant Pumpkin Mystery

Focus question: Where does a pumpkin's mass come from?

The phenomenon

A giant pumpkin seed weighs less than 1 gram. About four months after it is planted, the pumpkin can weigh more than 1,200 kilograms, about as much as a small car. On its fastest days, a giant pumpkin can gain about 20 kg in one day.

About 90% of a fresh pumpkin is water. The other 10%, the dry matter, is about 120 kg of sugars, fiber, proteins, fats, and other molecules.

Part 2: What are these things made of?

Everything is made of atoms. Each kind of atom is an element, like carbon (C), hydrogen (H), oxygen (O), nitrogen (N), or silicon (Si). If the pumpkin's matter came from a source, that source must contain the same elements.

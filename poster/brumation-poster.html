Charizard is in
Brumation!

That’s why our bearded dragon, Charizard, isn’t at school right now.

What is brumation?

Brumation is a long, deep rest that reptiles take when it gets cooler and the days get shorter. It’s like hibernation, but for reptiles. It is normal and healthy!

Why is it happening now?

🌤️

Shorter Days

Fall means less daylight. Charizard’s body notices and gets sleepy.

🌡️

Cooler Weather

Reptiles are cold-blooded. They get their heat from outside, not inside.

🐢

Slow Motion

Heartbeat and digestion slow way down to save energy until spring-like conditions return.

What it looks like

  • Sleeping a LOT, sometimes for days
  • Hiding or burrowing in a cozy spot
  • Eating little or nothing
  • Moving slowly, less interested in visitors
  • Pooping much less often

Why not at school?

  • Brumating dragons need a quiet, dim, calm place
  • Bells, noise, and bright lights can wake them up
  • Being handled or moved is stressful right now
  • Resting at a steady, cooler temperature keeps them safe
💚

Don’t worry — Charizard is okay!

Wild bearded dragons from Australia do this every year. She is being cared for and checked on, and will always have fresh water. If a pet ever loses weight or seems sick, a reptile vet can help.

When will she be back?

Charizard started in mid-October. Brumation usually lasts 1–3 months, so we expect her back between mid-December and January. It could take a little longer, and that’s okay!

    Three sources of new variation

    5 minRead, then write

    Scientists name three ways new genetic variation arises. Read all three, then fill in your notes.

    1. New combinations of alleles

    Sex cells carry one version of each gene, and fertilization puts two together. The cards showed this. An allele is one version of a gene. Letters like B and b stand for two alleles of one gene. A sex cell is an egg or a sperm.

    2. Copying errors

    DNA is copied before cells divide, and a rare error can slip through.

    3. Mutations from the environment

    UV light and some chemicals can damage DNA. A mutation is a change in the DNA.

    Day 1's idea 3 was about how an animal looks. Food, temperature, or a suntan can change how a body looks. That change is not in the DNA, so it is not inherited. UV light and some chemicals can change the DNA itself.
